Part 3 — The Truth

That evening, Daniel followed the map to the lighthouse. Behind a loose brick, he found another small key.

He used it to open a hidden compartment beneath the lighthouse stairs.

Inside was a box containing his grandfather's journal.

Daniel began reading and finally discovered the truth. His grandfather had been an explorer who had traveled across the world searching for a rare ancient artifact. Before he disappeared, he had hidden the artifact and created the clues so that only someone he trusted could find it.

At the bottom of the journal was one final message:

“The greatest treasure is not what you find, but the courage you gain while searching for it.”

Daniel smiled.

He had not only discovered his grandfather's secret—he had discovered something about himself.

From that day on, Daniel knew that whenever life placed a mysterious door in front of him, he would never be afraid to find out what was behind it.
