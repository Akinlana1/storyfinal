Part 2 — The Secret Room

Behind the door was a narrow staircase leading underground. At the bottom, Daniel found a room filled with old books, dusty maps, and a large wooden box.

On the box was a message:

“Only the person with a brave heart may open this.”

Daniel hesitated, then lifted the lid.

Inside was an old photograph of his grandfather, who had disappeared many years ago. Beside the photograph was a letter.

The letter read:

“Daniel, if you are reading this, you have found the secret I protected for years. Follow the map, and you will discover the truth.”

Daniel looked at the map. A red mark pointed toward the abandoned lighthouse outside town.

He knew he had to go there.
