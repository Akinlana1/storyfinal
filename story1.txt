The Mysterious Key
Part 1 — The Discovery

Daniel was walking home from school when he noticed something shining beneath an old tree. He bent down and picked it up. It was a small golden key with strange symbols carved into it.

That night, Daniel could not stop thinking about the key. The next morning, he returned to the tree and discovered a tiny wooden door hidden behind the roots.

He held the key against the lock.

Click.

The door slowly opened.

Daniel took a deep breath and stepped inside.
